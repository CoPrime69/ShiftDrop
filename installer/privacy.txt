ShiftDrop and your privacy

ShiftDrop works on your PC. It has no account, no telemetry and no ads, and it never uploads your files, your settings or anything about you.

The one thing that goes online is the update check. Once a day, a separate program, ShiftDrop-Updater.exe, asks GitHub for the latest ShiftDrop release, and when you choose to update it downloads that release's installer from GitHub. The request carries no cookies, no sign-in and no identifiers. Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address; the GitHub Privacy Statement covers how GitHub handles it.

You can turn the update check off on a later page of this setup, or at any time in ShiftDrop's Settings > Updates.

The full privacy policy: https://getshiftdrop.vercel.app/privacy.html
